Who is who

Rules that hold everywhere

  1. One shared record, the case. Every role sees the same case id and status through its own screen.
  2. The agent is the hub. Merchants never message staff directly, and staff reply inside the case thread.
  3. Humans act on decision packs the agent has already prepared, not on raw tickets.
  4. Every handoff is a database row, an audit entry and a notification.
  5. Money actions pass the policy check twice: when requested and when they run. Limits live in code.
  6. The Admin never works cases. The audit log can be read and exported, never edited.
